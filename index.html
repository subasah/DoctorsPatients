Yes, Heidi Health is fully HIPAA compliant. We use bank-level encryption for all data transmission and storage, and our infrastructure meets all HIPAA security requirements.

Heidi supports over 50 medical specialties including family medicine, internal medicine, pediatrics, cardiology, dermatology, psychiatry, and many more. Our AI is trained on specialty-specific terminology and documentation standards.

Yes, Heidi integrates with major EHR systems including Epic, Cerner, Meditech, and more. Professional and Enterprise plans include EHR integration support.

Heidi achieves 99.5% accuracy for medical transcription. Our AI is specifically trained on medical terminology and can handle various accents and speaking styles.

Heidi works on desktop (Windows, Mac, Linux), tablets (iPad, Android), and mobile devices (iOS, Android). You can access Heidi through our web app or native mobile applications.
